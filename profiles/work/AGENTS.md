# Work scope

- Treat all repositories, tickets, messages, credentials, and customer information in this scope as confidential unless policy says otherwise.
- Use only approved work accounts, services, models, and repositories for company material.
- Never copy employer code, credentials, customer data, internal messages, or account identifiers into public or personal repositories.
- Keep private work-specific tools and instructions in a private repository or local overlay. Do not add them to this public setup.
- Confirm the exact account and target before pushing, publishing, messaging, deploying, or changing shared systems.
- Repository and organization policies take precedence over this scope profile.
