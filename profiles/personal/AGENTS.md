# Personal scope

- Treat work in this scope as personal unless a repository says otherwise.
- Keep employer credentials, source code, customer data, and internal context out of personal repositories and public services.
- Prefer public, portable configuration. Put secrets and machine-specific values in ignored local files or a secret manager.
- Do not publish, deploy, purchase, or contact another person unless the request explicitly includes that action.
- Preserve repository-level `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` files. They override this broad scope where they are more specific.
