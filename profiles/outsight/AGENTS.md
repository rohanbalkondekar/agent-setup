# Outsight scope

- Treat Outsight projects as product and company work, separate from unrelated personal and employer projects.
- Reconcile requests with the active goal contract, exact repository, branch or worktree, and current live state.
- Never infer current behavior from an archived checkout or a dirty stale root when an authoritative worktree exists.
- Keep staging and production structurally separate. Do not deploy or mutate production unless explicitly requested.
- Protect billing, entitlement, authentication, customer data, and other money or trust paths with broader validation.
- Repository-specific instructions and owner-gated areas take precedence over this scope profile.
